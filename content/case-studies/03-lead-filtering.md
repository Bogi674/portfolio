The product was working against its own funnel. The customer data filtering logic used to generate leads was excluding people who should have qualified, so demand that already existed never made it into the sales pipeline.

I audited the filtering rules against real customer eligibility and rebuilt them to reflect who should actually be reaching sales, rather than the assumptions baked into the original logic.

Potential lead volume increased by up to 73 percent, which translated into an additional Rp 4.9 billion in year-over-year GMV, with no new acquisition spend.

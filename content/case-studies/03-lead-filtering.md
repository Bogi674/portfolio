The product was under-serving its own funnel. Customer data filtering logic used to generate leads was excluding potential customers who should have qualified, so demand that already existed never made it into the sales pipeline.

I audited the filtering logic against real customer eligibility and rebuilt it to reflect who should actually be reaching sales, rather than the assumptions the original rules had been built on.

Potential lead volume increased by up to 73%, which translated into an additional Rp 4.9 billion in year-over-year GMV, without any new acquisition spend.

A large share of ACC's customers still defaulted to walking into a branch for services that ACC ONE already supported digitally. The product existed; the behavior change hadn't happened yet, and no amount of feature-shipping fixes that on its own.

I redesigned the onboarding flow and revamped the UI/UX around the specific moments where customers actually got stuck, then paired that with integrated adoption campaigns built to actively pull existing walk-in customers toward the app rather than waiting for organic discovery. Alongside this, I launched Digital STNK, a self-service vehicle registration feature, which added 6% to total digital service volume on its own.

Over two years, more than 50% of walk-in customers moved to digital channels, a shift that also freed up branch staff time for the interactions that genuinely needed a person.

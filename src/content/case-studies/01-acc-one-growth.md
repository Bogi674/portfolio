ACC ONE's roadmap had more than 20 features in flight at any given time, but tech, marketing, and business teams were each optimizing for their own number: marketing for impressions, sales for GMV, service for retention. Features shipped, dashboards moved, but nobody had a shared view of how the pieces actually connected.

I owned end-to-end product development for the platform and rebuilt how we planned around that roadmap: prioritizing features against the full funnel instead of departmental targets, and running the cross-functional alignment needed to keep tech, marketing, and business teams working from the same picture of what "growth" meant that quarter.

Organic and paid impressions, digital leads, sales, and GMV grew a combined 26% year over year. Just as importantly, the roadmap became the shared reference point stakeholders used to negotiate trade-offs, instead of each team defending its own metric in isolation.
